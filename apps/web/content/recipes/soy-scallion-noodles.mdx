A few notes: the sauce is intense, so start small — spoon just a little over each
individual portion and add more to taste. Pat the scallions thoroughly dry before
frying to cut down on splattering, and julienne them into short, thin strips so
they crisp up. Leftover sauce keeps in the fridge for a faster meal later.

A few notes: Snyder's gluten-free pretzels are crunchier than the regular ones if
you can find them. Furikake usually lives in the Asian aisle or any major Asian
grocer (Amazon too). Large jelly roll pans (about 15x20 in) or half sheet pans
(about 13x18 in) both work — just stir halfway so the glaze coats evenly, and give
everything one more stir while it cools to stop clumping.

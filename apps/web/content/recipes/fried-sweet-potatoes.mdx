  - { text: "Cut like a pro: peel (optional, but recommended for max crisp), then cut into 1/2-inch batons like steak fries. Keep the sizes consistent — this matters." }
  - { text: "Parboil (do not skip): drop the fries into cold, well-salted water, bring to a gentle boil, and cook 4-5 minutes until just barely tender (a knife meets slight resistance). Drain immediately.", durationSeconds: 270 }
  - { text: "Steam-dry and starch coat: spread the fries on a tray, let the steam evaporate 5-7 minutes, then toss gently with the cornstarch and seasonings until lightly powdery and dry." }
  - { text: "First fry (the setup): heat the oil to 325°F and fry in small batches 3-4 minutes — no browning yet, just structure. Remove to a rack and rest 10 minutes.", durationSeconds: 210 }

To make bunnies: tint 1 tsp of the dough pink. Roll a small white ball for the tail
and attach it to the back of a tang yuan with a dab of water. Form two little logs
for ears and top them with a bit of pink, press black sesame seeds in for eyes, and
add a tiny pink dot for the nose.
